Who can apply

The event is open to all Kuchipudi artists above six years of age. Applications and registrations are submitted by individual performers, gurus, or institutions on behalf of their students.

Dates to remember

Applications close
30 November 2026
Reporting time
10:00 AM on 27 December 2026

What participants need to know

  • What to prepare. Every artist memorises, recites and performs abhinaya for the Natya Shastra slokas: Natya Vedam, Asamyuta Hasta, Samyuta Hasta and Nritta Hasta. These open the event.
  • Learning material. Reference videos are shared once registration and payment of the fee are complete.
  • Dress. Traditional Kuchipudi costume. Participants arrange their own make-up.
  • Recognition. Each participant in this Kuchipudi world record event 2026 receives an official certificate and a memento. Natya gurus are felicitated.
  • Food. Meals are provided on the day of the event for each performing artist and one accompanying person.
  • Fee. The application fee and how to pay it are given in the form. Read the refund terms there before you pay.

The form carries the complete terms and conditions. Where this page and the form differ, the form applies.

Part of the Dance Confluence

Geeta Saaram Natya Vedam takes place on 27 December 2026, the final day of the International Kuchipudi Dance Confluence.
