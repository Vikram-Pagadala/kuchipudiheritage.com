Kuchipudi Natya Pathaka Swarnotsavalu, 27 to 29 December 2024

Fifty years after the Kuchipudi flag was created, the Society hosted its golden jubilee in Kuchipudi village: an international gathering of Kuchipudi dancers, teachers and scholars over three days.

  • Morning lectures by senior gurus and scholars on the history of Kuchipudi and its growth as a solo form around the world.
  • A photo exhibition on the Kuchipudi dance-dramas of the Bhagavata tradition.
  • Performances from late morning into the night, by artists and groups from across India and from the USA, Canada, France and Russia.
  • Tributes to veterans of the art, among them Chinta Venkataramayya, Vempati Chinna Satyam and Vedantam Parvateesam.
  • A 50-foot stupa raised in the village, with the Kuchipudi flag hoisted on it.

The jubilee was also marked in Washington, D.C., in a programme presented with the Embassy of India and Kuchipudi Kala Nilayam.
