Our eyes, kohl-rimmed and expressive, narrate the epics of the Ramayana and the Mahabharata. Our hands weave a tapestry of emotions: the playful sway of a gopi yearning for Krishna, the fierce defiance of Draupadi wronged, the unwavering devotion of a bhakta.

That is how the old masters of Kuchipudi are remembered in the village: as living repositories of a tradition handed down through generations. Most of them belonged to the hereditary families of Kuchipudi. They began as child actors in the dance-drama troupes, played heroines and heroes as they grew, and ended as teachers.

For the story they belong to, read the history of Kuchipudi dance.
