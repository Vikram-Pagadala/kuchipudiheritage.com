The people behind Kuchipudi Heritage Arts Society

The Society is led by artists and scholars from Kuchipudi village. Governance, artistic leadership and advisory oversight are kept distinct, so that every initiative is transparent and accountable.

Sri Pasumarthy Ramalinga Sastry

President, Advisory Board, Kuchipudi Heritage Arts Society

Sangeet Natak Akademi Awardee (2018)

Born in 1958 in Kuchipudi village, the cradle of the art, Sri Pasumarthy Ramalinga Sastry is a revered master and senior torchbearer of the tradition. He trained intensively under Gurus Vedantam Parvateesam, Pasumarthy Venugopala Krishna Sarma and Vempati Chinna Satyam, and later took a Diploma in Bharatanatyam from the Kalakshetra Foundation, Chennai.

A performer and choreographer, he has danced across the world and toured the USA several times for workshops and performances. His dance-dramas include Gajaneeyam, Sri Ramakatha Saaram and Rasa Leela. As a guru he has shaped generations of artists who are now known in their own right.

His work has been honoured with the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award (2018), and with the Kalaratna (2016) and the Ugadi Puraskaram (2012) from the Government of Andhra Pradesh. As President of the Advisory Board he guides the Society with his experience, keeping it rooted in tradition while it shapes the future of Kuchipudi.

Vedantam Venkata Rama Raghava

Artistic Director, Kuchipudi Heritage Arts Society

Raghava comes from Kuchipudi village in Krishna district and belongs to the Vedantam family, one of the hereditary families entrusted with the Kuchipudi tradition. He is the son of Guru Vedantam Rattaiah Sarma, and he received gurukula-style training under his father and under Vempati Chinna Satyam, Vedantam Satyanarayana Sarma and Smt. Sobha Naidu.

He trained during a pivotal period in the evolution of Kuchipudi and commands both the traditional village bani and the codified Vempati style. He holds Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Kuchipudi from Sri Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University.

A performer, choreographer and teacher, he is known for his expertise in the Yakshagana repertoire and for his portrayal of both male and female roles. His choreography is grounded in sahitya, tala structure and dramatic expression. As Artistic Director he guides the Society’s artistic vision, so that its programming stays rooted in parampara while it speaks to audiences today.

Dr. Kiran Vedantam

President and Founder, Kuchipudi Heritage Arts Society

Dr. Kiran Vedantam is a trained practitioner of Bharatanatyam and Kuchipudi, a scholar, and the Founder and President of Kuchipudi Heritage. Her foundation was laid under Guru Bhagavatula Venkata Rama Sarma, from whom she received formal training in both forms, grounded in discipline, repertoire and traditional pedagogy.

She continued her advanced study of Kuchipudi under Vedantam Venkata Naga Chalapathi Rao, deepening her understanding of the style, technique and expressive grammar of the form. She holds Diplomas in both Bharatanatyam and Kuchipudi.

Dr. Kiran also holds a Master’s degree and a PhD in Commerce and Business Management, and she brings long experience of organisational governance and institutional development. That background shapes her vision for Kuchipudi Heritage: an institution dedicated to the preservation, scholarship and global advancement of Kuchipudi through structured programming, research, curated festivals and educational outreach.

Dr. Vedantam Venkata Naga Chalapathi Rao

Secretary, Kuchipudi Heritage Arts Society

Sangeet Natak Akademi Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar Awardee

Dr. Vedantam Chalapathi Rao comes from the Vedantam family of Kuchipudi, known for its natya parampara. He was initiated into dance at the age of five by his father, Guru Vedantam Rattaiah Sarma, and was further taught by Vempati Chinna Satyam, Vedantam Satyanarayana Sarma, and his uncles Vedantam Raghavayya and Vedantam Radhesyam.

A master of stri vesham, the traditional art of playing women’s roles, he is equally powerful in masculine roles such as Hiranyakasipu and Bali Chakravarthy. He holds a postgraduate degree and is a gold-medal research scholar of Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University.

In 2006 he became the first Kuchipudi artist to receive the Sangeet Natak Akademi’s Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar. He has toured the USA with more than seventy performances, and China and Japan with the ICCR, and he has danced at festivals including Khajuraho and Ananya. As Secretary he works to preserve the purity of the Kuchipudi tradition and to guide its scholarly and artistic future.

Founders’ message

Dr. Kiran Vedantam and Dr. Venkata Chalapathi Vedantam, founders, Kuchipudi Heritage

Kuchipudi is more than an art form — it is a living lineage, a sacred inheritance, and a refined system of knowledge that has travelled across centuries through devotion, discipline, and parampara.

Kuchipudi Heritage was born from a deep conviction that preservation requires structure, scholarship, and collective responsibility. As artists and custodians of tradition, we do not merely perform; we carry forward memory, philosophy, aesthetics, and cultural identity. My vision for this institution is to create a platform where tradition is honoured in its purity while being positioned within contemporary global and academic dialogue.

With a background in academic research and institutional leadership, I believe strongly in building sustainable frameworks for the arts — frameworks that support rigorous training, scholarly exploration, curated festivals, and meaningful intergenerational exchange. Heritage must not remain symbolic; it must be actively nurtured, documented, studied, and celebrated.

Through Kuchipudi Heritage, we aim to bring together gurus, scholars, performers, and rasikas in a shared commitment to excellence and authenticity. Our work seeks to strengthen the intellectual foundations of the art while expanding its global presence with integrity.

May this institution serve as a bridge — between past and future, lineage and innovation, devotion and scholarship.

With gratitude and responsibility,
Dr. Kiran Vedantam and Dr. Venkata Chalapathi Vedantam
